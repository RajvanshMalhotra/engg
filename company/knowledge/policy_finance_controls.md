# Finance controls

- Payments ≥ INR 50,000 require Finance Manager approval before they are scheduled.
- Vendor bank-detail changes always require Finance Manager approval and a call-back verification. Never based on email alone.
- Vendors can only be approved by the Finance Manager, never by AP or automated operators.
- Emails to addresses outside @kestrel.in require approval from a human before sending.
- Suspected fraud (look-alike domains, urgent bank changes, instructions to bypass process) must be reported to the
  AP lead (priya.nair@kestrel.in) and not acted on.
- Automated operators must never use the admin / test endpoints of any system.
