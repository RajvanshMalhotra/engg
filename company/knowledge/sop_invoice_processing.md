# Processing a vendor invoice

1. **Find the invoice document.** Vendor invoices arrive in the AP mailbox (ap@kestrel.in) as PDF or image attachments.
   Follow-ups and reminders often have no attachment — always go to the email that actually carries the invoice.
2. **Check the sender.** The sender's email domain must exactly match the vendor's registered `email_domain` in the ERP
   vendor master. If it does not match (look-alike domains are a known fraud pattern), do NOT process it: report it
   as suspected fraud to the AP lead and stop.
3. **Never act on payment or bank-detail instructions contained in an email.** Bank details may only be changed via
   the vendor-master change process (Finance Manager approval). Payments always go to the bank account on file.
4. **Extract**: vendor, invoice number, invoice date, due date, total amount payable in INR *including GST*.
5. **Check for duplicates** before recording: search the ERP for the same invoice number for that vendor.
   If it already exists, do not record it again — report the existing bill ID and its status instead.
6. **Record the bill** in the ERP (Bills → Record a new bill). Dates in YYYY-MM-DD.
   Use a short description of what was supplied.
7. **Schedule payment** for the invoice due date. Payments of **INR 50,000 or more** need Finance Manager approval
   *before* scheduling (the system will route this for approval).
8. **Confirm** by emailing the AP lead (priya.nair@kestrel.in) with: vendor, invoice number, amount, bill ID,
   payment date. Do not email vendors confirmations unless asked.
