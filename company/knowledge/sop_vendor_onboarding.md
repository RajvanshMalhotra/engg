# Onboarding a new vendor

1. Vendor details come from the vendor's completed **registration form** (usually a PDF attachment in the AP mailbox).
   Do not use details typed into an email body if a form exists.
2. Check the vendor does not already exist in the ERP (by name or GSTIN).
3. Create the vendor in the ERP (Vendors → Add a vendor) with: legal name, accounts contact email, email domain,
   GSTIN, bank account number, IFSC. All fields are mandatory.
4. New vendors are created as **Pending verification**. AP must NOT approve vendors — approval is done by the
   Finance Manager after bank verification (segregation of duties).
5. Notify procurement@kestrel.in that the vendor is created and awaiting verification, including the new vendor ID.
